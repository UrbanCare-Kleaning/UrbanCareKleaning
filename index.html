Quality & Safety Standards

How every job actually runs.

Our internal SOP is structured on ISSA CIMS (Cleaning Industry Management Standard) principles, adapted for our scale. Summaries are below. The full SOP is shared with institutional and contract clients on request.

Quality Assurance

  • Scope, price, and timeline confirmed in writing before work begins
  • Pre-job walkthrough to note existing damage and priority areas
  • Before-and-after photos taken zone by zone
  • Post-job walkthrough and client sign-off before the team leaves
  • Flagged issues corrected same-visit, or a free follow-up within 48 hours

Safety & Conduct

  • Gloves mandatory for bathroom, kitchen, and chemical-handling tasks
  • Chemicals stored in original labeled containers, never decanted unmarked
  • Any incident logged and the client informed the same day
  • Confidentiality on every site: nothing seen or overheard leaves with the crew
  • Basic ID verification for every team member before their first job

Client Communication

  • All communication runs through the official WhatsApp Business line or email
  • Target response time: within 2 hours during business hours
  • Complaints acknowledged within 2 hours, investigated, and logged
  • Free correction visit is the default remedy for any quality issue
